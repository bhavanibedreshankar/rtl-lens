Hypothesis / root causertl/dma/tpe_dma.sv:239
Confidence
61%

In the ST_WR_DATA state, m_wdata is driven directly from the combinational sram_rdata port (line 231: assign m_wdata = sram_rdata;), relying on dp_ram's registered read port to hold the beat fetched during the single-cycle ST_WR_SRAM_RD state stable until the write happens. However the SRAM read enable (sram_en) is only asserted while state_q==ST_WR_SRAM_RD, a single cycle, and is de-asserted once the FSM moves to ST_WR_DATA. If the write side (m_wready) stalls (not immediately ready), the FSM remains in ST_WR_DATA for more than one cycle while sram_en is low, and depending on the dp_ram model's port behavero when not enabled, sram_rdata will not continue to hold correctly (e.g. may go to 0 due to enable-gated output register), producing an eventual all-zero payload as seen in mismatch row 16 (rtl=0x0...0, expected pattern). The minimal fix is to keep sram_en held (re-issuing the same-address read, harmless since sram_we stays 0) throughout ST_WR_DATA whenever the write beat hasn't completed yet, ensuring the registered sram_rdata continues to reflect the same row's data until the AXI write actually completes.
